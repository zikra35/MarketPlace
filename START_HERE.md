## ❓ FAQ

**Q: How long does deployment take?**
A: About 30 minutes total. Most time is spent waiting for builds.

**Q: Will it cost money?**
A: Railway ($5/month) + MongoDB free tier = $5/month minimum. Vercel is free.

**Q: Can I deploy to other platforms?**
A: Yes! The code works on AWS, Heroku, DigitalOcean, etc. But Railway+Vercel is easiest.

**Q: Do I need a custom domain?**
A: No, you'll get free Railway and Vercel domains. Add custom domain later if needed.

**Q: How do I monitor the app after deployment?**
A: Each platform has built-in logs and monitoring. See DEPLOYMENT_CHECKLIST.md
